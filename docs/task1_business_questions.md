# Task 1: Framing the Business Scenario

### CEO Focus Questions
1. What is the monthly revenue trend for the year 2011 to spot market growth patterns?
2. Which are the top 10 countries generating the highest revenue and quantity sold to evaluate operational footprint?
3. How is the geographical revenue distributed across global regions (excluding the UK) to plan strategic investments?
4. Which product stock codes map to peak global demands to ensure seamless inventory management?

### CMO Focus Questions
1. Who are the top 10 most valuable customers by revenue contribution to build a dedicated loyalty program?
2. What are the seasonal peaks in product sales to optimize seasonal advertising campaigns?
3. Are there specific countries with low demand requiring targeted regional promotions?
4. What is the average order value (AOV) across the highest-performing consumer markets?
