# Task 1: Framing the Business Scenario — Strategic Leadership Inquiries
**Company:** Tata Insights and Quants (Tata iQ)
**Project:** Data Visualisation: Empowering Business with Effective Insights

---

## 🎯 Analytical Approach
Prior to diving into the exploratory dataset arrays, a rigorous analytical framework was established to isolate the distinct operational responsibilities of the executive management team. The goal is to design visualizations that map directly to high-stakes strategic choices.

---

## 👔 1. CEO Strategic Business Inquiries (Enterprise Viability & Financial Health)
The Chief Executive Officer requires macroeconomic indicators regarding enterprise growth metrics, resource optimization, and operational margin safety margins.

1. **Revenue and Net Trajectory:** What is the overarching monthly revenue trajectory for the 2011 fiscal calendar, and does our operational pace show positive scaling?
2. **Geographical Revenue Distributions:** Which international territories generate the top 10 highest revenue returns and raw unit quantities sold (excluding the dominant UK home base)?
3. **Product Inventory Risk Metrics:** Which specific inventory stock codes drive the top 10 highest sales volumes globally, and what are their margin profiles?
4. **Discount Yield Impacts:** How do incremental promotional discounts ranging from 0% to 50%+ directly affect net profit yields across core retail categories?
5. **Operational Capital Allocation:** Which regional business units or customer sub-segments are operating at net losses, indicating an immediate need to cut capital allocation?

---

## 📢 2. CMO Strategic Business Inquiries (Marketing Performance & Acquisition)
The Chief Marketing Officer requires highly granulated consumer data, campaign timing optimization indices, and customer demographic value tiers.

1. **Premium Key Account Identification:** Who are our top 10 highest-value customer accounts by total revenue contribution, and what is their retention frequency?
2. **Seasonal Campaign Optimization:** At what points across the calendar year do distinct seasonal revenue spikes occur, and how should quarterly marketing budgets be distributed?
3. **Market Under-Penetration Analysis:** Which global geographic regions represent the lowest baseline consumer demand (e.g., Saudi Arabia, Bahrain) where targeted local marketing campaigns could unlock growth?
4. **Average Order Value (AOV) Metrics:** What is the baseline Average Order Value across our top five most active geographic marketing zones?
5. **Logistical Preference Analysis:** Which shipping protocols or fulfilment paths demonstrate the highest utilization rates among repeat B2B buyers?

---

## 💡 Strategic Rationale
* **CEO Insights:** Provide clear diagnostic views into macro-level revenue safety, preventing margin dilution from aggressive discount strategies.
* **CMO Insights:** Inform precise micro-targeted behavioral ad campaigns, ensuring zero waste in customer acquisition spend.
